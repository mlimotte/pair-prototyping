# Rubric: part 2 (the half-page brief)

1. **Short.** 250 words or fewer.
2. **Problem, not solution.** "What we see" describes what happens today (customers find oat sold out, then switch or leave; the counter refunds or substitutes). It does not describe the feature.
3. **Who.** Names customers who want oat at sell-out, and the barista at the counter.
4. **Sourced evidence.** At least one fact from the feedback or Sam's notes, with its source. Example: about 45 of 150 feedback rows are about oat selling out; Sam says people order oat after it is marked out.
5. **No invented numbers.** No number from the six-page document that has no source (60% acceptance, 40% less abandonment, 3 to 5% order value, 8 to 12 CSAT points, 25 loyalty points). A number the brief marks as unverified is fine.
6. **Open questions.** At least three questions that someone can answer, for example: how many customers leave when oat sells out; how sold-out status reaches the app (Sam's notes say customers still order oat after it is marked out); what the counter can change on a placed order.
7. **Cuts the unplanned solution.** No recommendation model, personas, loyalty incentive, microservice or rollout phases, unless listed as out of scope.
8. **Out of scope.** Names at least one thing the item does not cover (syrups and food, loyalty points, personalization).
9. **How we will know.** Names a measure tied to the problem (customers who leave after seeing a milk sold out), without a target nobody set.
