# Notes from the counter (Sam, Pearl Street)

Things that slow us down, in no order.

- Oat runs out most afternoons in summer. When I mark it out on the board, people still order it in the app for a few minutes after. Then I have to tell them at the counter. Some want a refund, some take almond. Nobody is happy.
- When I make it with almond instead, the ticket still says oat. Inventory counts are off at the end of the week.
- Morning rush: the pickup shelf gets full. People ask "is mine ready?" while it's sitting there.
- People ask if we can save their usual drink.
- We tried editing tickets at the counter back in March. That was the double-charge mess. We stopped.
- The app should tell people oat is out BEFORE they get to the counter. I thought it already did?
